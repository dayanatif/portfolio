About

Researching models beyond their final prediction.

My current work is centered on mechanistic questions: what individual neurons encode, when those representations matter, and how causal interventions can validate an interpretation rather than merely describe a correlation.

I am an undergraduate Computer Engineering student at Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology (SSUET), Pakistan, and a 2026 Mitacs Globalink Research Intern at Dalhousie University in Halifax, Canada.

At Dalhousie, I work under Prof. Hassan Sajjad on explainable AI, with a current focus on neuron-level interpretation supported by causal intervention. The broader goal is to study not only whether a concept is represented inside a model, but whether the model actually uses that representation during its computation.

I also enjoy building practical machine-learning systems across language, vision, retrieval-augmented generation, adversarial robustness, and human-computer interaction. My long-term goal is graduate research in trustworthy and interpretable AI.
